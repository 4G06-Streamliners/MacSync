\chapter{Introduction}

\section{About MacSync}

MacSync is an event management platform built for the McMaster Engineering Society (MES).
It gives McMaster students a single place to discover events, purchase tickets, and
manage their registrations --- all through a dedicated mobile app available on iOS
and Android.

\section{Who This Guide Is For}

This guide is for \textbf{Members} --- any registered McMaster student using the
MacSync mobile app to browse and attend events. If you are a staff member or
administrator, refer to the \textit{MacSync Staff \& Admin Guide}.

\section{How to Use This Guide}

Read Chapter~\ref{chap:started} to get set up, then jump to whichever section
covers what you need. Callout boxes highlight important information:

\begin{notebox}
Notes provide extra context or background information.
\end{notebox}

\begin{warnbox}
Warnings flag actions that could have unintended consequences.
\end{warnbox}

\begin{tipbox}
Tips share best practices and useful shortcuts.
\end{tipbox}

\section{System Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item iOS or Android smartphone with the MacSync app installed.
  \item Active internet connection.
  \item A valid \texttt{@mcmaster.ca} email address.
\end{itemize}


\chapter{Getting Started}
\label{chap:started}

\section{Downloading the App}

The MacSync mobile app is available on both platforms:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{iOS:} Search \textit{MacSync} on the Apple App Store.
  \item \textbf{Android:} Search \textit{MacSync} on the Google Play Store.
\end{itemize}

\section{Creating an Account}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Open the MacSync app.
  \item Tap \uielem{Sign Up}.
  \item Enter your \texttt{@mcmaster.ca} email address.
  \item A \textbf{verification code} will be sent to your McMaster inbox.
  \item Enter the verification code in the app to confirm your email.
  \item Complete your profile and set a password.
  \item Tap \uielem{Create Account}.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{notebox}
Only valid \texttt{@mcmaster.ca} email addresses can register on MacSync.
Non-McMaster emails will not receive a verification code.
\end{notebox}

\section{Logging In}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Open the MacSync app.
  \item Enter your \texttt{@mcmaster.ca} email and password.
  \item Tap \uielem{Sign In}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Navigating the App}

Once logged in, the bottom navigation bar gives you access to four sections:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \uielem{Events} --- Browse all upcoming open events and sign up.
  \item \uielem{My Tickets} --- View your active and past tickets.
  \item \uielem{Notifications} --- View event announcements and updates.
  \item \uielem{Profile} --- Manage your account information.
\end{itemize}

\section{Logging Out}

Tap \uielem{Logout} in the top-right corner of the app. You will be returned
to the login screen.


\chapter{Browsing \& Registering for Events}

\section{Browsing Events}

The \uielem{Events} tab shows all upcoming open events. Each event card displays:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Event name, date, and location.
  \item Number of registered attendees.
  \item Ticket price (or \textit{Free} if no charge).
  \item An \textbf{Open} badge if registration is currently available.
\end{itemize}

Use the search bar at the top to find a specific event by name.

\section{Registering for a Free Event}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Tap an event card to view its details.
  \item If the event has table or bus signup enabled, you will be prompted
        to select your preferred seat before confirming.
  \item Tap \uielem{Sign Up} to complete registration.
\end{enumerate}

Once registered, the event card will show a \textbf{\checkmark\ Signed Up} badge.

\section{Registering for a Paid Event}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Tap an event card to view its details.
  \item If seat selection is required, choose your table or bus seat.
  \item Tap \uielem{Proceed to payment}.
  \item The \textbf{Stripe checkout screen} will appear within the app.
        You can pay using:
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Link} --- Stripe's saved payment method for faster checkout.
      \item \textbf{Card} --- Enter your card number, expiry, CVV,
            cardholder name, and country.
    \end{itemize}
  \item Tap \uielem{Pay} to complete your purchase.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{notebox}
Your \texttt{@mcmaster.ca} email is pre-filled in the Stripe checkout form.
You can optionally save your card via Stripe Link for faster checkout next time.
\end{notebox}

\section{Table \& Bus Seat Selection}

Some events require you to choose a seat as part of registration:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Table signup} --- Select your preferred table number and seat
        at that table. There is no visual seating map; selection is by number.
  \item \textbf{Bus signup} --- Select your preferred bus number and seat on
        that bus.
\end{itemize}

Your seat selection is shown on your ticket after registration.


\chapter{My Tickets}


\section{Viewing Your Tickets}

Tap \uielem{My Tickets} in the bottom navigation bar. Tickets are organised
into two tabs:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \uielem{Current} --- Active upcoming tickets.
  \item \uielem{Past \& Refunded} --- Tickets for past events or processed refunds.
\end{itemize}

Each ticket card shows the event name, date, location, price, and your
seat assignment (if applicable).

\section{Your Entry QR Code}

Tap \uielem{View details} on any ticket to open the full ticket page, which includes:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Event location, price, and seat assignment.
  \item An \textbf{Entry QR Code} --- show this to event staff at the entrance
        for check-in.
  \item A \textbf{Request Refund} button.
  \item Your unique \textbf{Ticket ID}.
\end{itemize}

\begin{tipbox}
Save a screenshot of your QR code before the event in case you lose
internet connection on the day.
\end{tipbox}

\section{Requesting a Refund}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Go to \uielem{My Tickets} and tap the relevant ticket.
  \item Tap \uielem{View details}.
  \item Tap \uielem{Request Refund} (shown in red).
\end{enumerate}

Once processed, the ticket will move to the \uielem{Past \& Refunded} tab.

\begin{warnbox}
Refund eligibility may depend on the event organiser's policy. Contact the
organiser directly if your refund request is not processed.
\end{warnbox}


\chapter{Notifications}

\section{Receiving Notifications}

MacSync sends notifications to keep you informed about events you are
registered for. You will receive them in two ways:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Push notification} on your device (if notifications are
        enabled for the MacSync app in your phone settings).
  \item \textbf{In-app} under the \uielem{Notifications} tab, which shows
        a badge count for unread items.
\end{itemize}

\section{Types of Notifications}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Ticket confirmation} --- Sent automatically when you
        successfully register for an event
        (e.g.\ \textit{``You're registered for [Event]! Your ticket has
        been confirmed.''}).
  \item \textbf{Event announcements} --- Sent by event staff with updates
        or reminders (e.g.\ \textit{``Remember to bring your student ID!''}).
\end{itemize}

Notifications appear in reverse chronological order. Unread ones are marked
with a dot. Tap \uielem{Mark All Read} to clear all unread indicators.


\chapter{Account Settings}

\section{Viewing Your Profile}

Tap \uielem{Profile} in the bottom navigation bar to view your account
information:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Name and role (e.g.\ Member).
  \item Email address (cannot be changed).
  \item Phone number.
  \item McMaster program.
  \item In-App Notifications toggle.
\end{itemize}

\section{Editing Your Profile}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Tap \uielem{Profile}, then tap \uielem{Edit Profile}.
  \item Update any of the following:
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Name
      \item Phone number
      \item Program
      \item In-App Notifications toggle
    \end{itemize}
  \item Tap \uielem{Save Changes} to apply, or \uielem{Cancel} to discard.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{notebox}
Your \textbf{email address} is linked to your McMaster account and
cannot be changed after registration.
\end{notebox}

\section{Notification Preferences}

The \uielem{In-App Notifications} toggle controls whether you receive push
notifications from MacSync:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{On} --- Notifications are delivered to your device and
        visible in the \uielem{Notifications} tab.
  \item \textbf{Off} --- Notifications are silenced, but you can still
        view them manually in the \uielem{Notifications} tab.
\end{itemize}


\chapter{Glossary}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=3.5cm, style=sameline]
  \item[Check-in]      The process of having your QR code scanned at the
                       event entrance to confirm your attendance.
  \item[Entry QR Code] A unique scannable code on your ticket used for
                       event check-in.
  \item[Event]         A scheduled activity organised by an MES club or
                       society, visible to all MacSync members.
  \item[Member]        The default role for every registered MacSync user.
  \item[MES]           McMaster Engineering Society --- the organisation
                       that operates the MacSync platform.
  \item[Stripe]        The payment processor used by MacSync for paid
                       event registrations.
  \item[Ticket ID]     A unique identifier assigned to each ticket purchase.
\end{description}
